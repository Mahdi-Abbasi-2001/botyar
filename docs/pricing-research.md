# Pricing research (looked up 2026-10-05, for the business plan)

All numbers below are copied from the pages named; nothing is estimated. Re-check before quoting in the pitch (pages change).

## Competitor: SahBot (صهبات) — sahbot.com
- Usage-based "diamonds" (الماس) and "points" (امتیاز): a new user gets **2,000 diamonds + 6,000 points free** for the first bot.
- The bot's **daily diamond use is computed from the features/processes added** (docs example: «پل ارتباطی» 20 diamonds/day; «حذف تبلیغات» 10 diamonds per type).
- **Toman price per diamond / purchase packages are NOT public** on sahbot.com or its docs page; only visible inside the dashboard (web.sahbot.com) or from support. → Open item: sign up and read the payment page, or ask support.
- Sources: https://sahbot.com/ , https://sahbot.com/doc.html

## Competitor: AradBot (آرادبات) — flat monthly subscription per plan (VAT extra)
| Plan | Monthly | Annual (list / discounted) | Bots | Notable limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trial | free 7 days | – | 1 | full features |
| Shell (صدف) | 183,000 Toman | 2,196,000 / 1,713,000 | 1 | 200 buttons, 3 operators, 4 groups/channels |
| Pearl (مروارید) | 287,000 Toman | 3,444,000 / 2,686,000 | 2 | 1,000 buttons, 10 operators, 12 groups/channels |
| Island (جزیره) | 609,000 Toman | 7,308,000 / 5,700,000 | 3 | unlimited users, 5,000 buttons |
- Takeaway: a **per-plan monthly subscription with annual discounts (~22–26% off)** already exists in this market, which supports the model we proposed.
- Source: https://aradbot.com/feature/

## What a freelancer/agency charges to BUILD a bot (filtori.com, 1405)
- Bale — informational bot: **2–8 million Toman**; e-commerce bot: **12–30 million**; custom: **from 30 million**.
- Telegram — informational: 3–11 million; e-commerce: 15–45 million; custom: from 35 million.
- Extra features (payment gateway, AI support, booking system, external APIs, bulk messaging) each add roughly **1.5–7 million Toman**.
- Takeaway: building is a one-off cost of millions of Toman; our agent builds and changes a bot in minutes for about $0.003 of AI cost. This is the "10x cheaper than a freelancer" argument — use these numbers, not invented ones.
- Source: https://filtori.com/tools/ (a calculator/price guide page; one source, treat as indicative)
